Questions businesses ask before hiring an SEO agency in Limassol

Clear answers help you understand the investment, timescale and scope before choosing a partner.

How much does SEO cost in Limassol?

The cost depends on your website, competition, target services and whether the work includes technical fixes, local SEO, content or ongoing delivery. We review the starting point first, then recommend a clear scope rather than selling the same package to every business.

How long does SEO take?

Technical and profile improvements can be implemented quickly, but meaningful organic growth usually takes time. The timeline depends on the website, competition, authority, content and speed of implementation. We set measurable priorities without promising a fixed ranking date.

Can SEO help my business appear on Google Maps?

Yes. Local SEO can improve the relevance and completeness of your Google Business Profile, business information, reviews process and website connection. Maps positions still vary by searcher location, relevance, prominence and Google's systems, so no exact position can be guaranteed.

Do you work with businesses across Cyprus?

Yes. Studio 17 operates from Limassol and supports businesses targeting customers locally and across Cyprus. For broader national search priorities, see our SEO services in Cyprus.

What SEO services do you provide in Limassol?

We provide technical SEO, on-page optimisation, local SEO, Google Business Profile support, search-led content, Search Console measurement and AI search/GEO. Explore our complete SEO capability for the full scope.

Do I need a new website before starting SEO?

Not always. We first assess whether the current website can support the technical, content and conversion changes required. If it can, we prioritise improvements; if it cannot, we explain the limitation before recommending a rebuild.

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No responsible SEO agency can guarantee a position controlled by a search engine. We commit to evidence-based priorities, careful implementation and transparent measurement of visibility and business actions.

What is included in the free SEO analysis?

It is an initial review of your business context, website, visible technical and local-search foundations, and the route from discovery to contact. It helps us identify the most useful next investigation and recommend an appropriate scope.
