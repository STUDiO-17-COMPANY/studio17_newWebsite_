Questions Limassol businesses ask before starting local SEO.

These answers explain what local SEO can change, what it cannot guarantee and where the work normally begins.

What is the difference between local SEO and general SEO?

Local SEO focuses on searches where location affects the choice. It connects Google Business Profile, Maps, accurate business information, local relevance and location-focused website pages. General SEO can target broader informational and commercial demand without a strong proximity signal.

Can you optimise our Google Business Profile and Maps presence?

Yes. We can review categories, services, business information, profile completeness, website connections and the genuine review process. Visibility still depends on Google and the competitive context, so no specific Maps position is guaranteed.

Does my business need a Limassol landing page?

Only when the page represents a real service and provides useful local information. A thin page that simply repeats another location is not a good strategy. We first assess search intent, customer need and the role the page would play.

How long does local SEO take?

It depends on the starting profile, website, competition, review signals and the changes required. Some profile corrections can be implemented quickly, but meaningful visibility and enquiry growth usually require ongoing observation and improvement.

Can you guarantee the top position in the Google map results?

No. Map results vary by search, location, relevance, prominence and Google systems. We improve the factors the business can responsibly influence and measure the resulting visibility and actions.

Can local SEO work if we serve Limassol without a storefront?

Potentially, yes. Service-area businesses can configure their presence differently from customer-facing premises. We assess eligibility, profile setup and the website's service-area information before recommending changes.

How important are Google reviews for local SEO?

Genuine reviews help customers evaluate a business and contribute to its local presence. We can help design a consistent request-and-response process, but we do not create, buy or manipulate reviews.

What is included in the free local SEO analysis?

It is an initial review of the business context, website, visible profile foundations and local search journey. We use it to identify the appropriate next investigation and explain whether a focused fix or a wider programme is more suitable.
