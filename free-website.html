Questions before applying.

Know what the offer covers, what it does not cover and what happens after you apply.

Is the website really free?

For selected businesses, Studio 17 waives the design and development fee for the agreed one-page scope. External costs and work outside that scope are not included.

Who can apply for a free website?

Credible businesses and projects that need a professional first website may apply. An application does not guarantee selection.

What do I need to provide?

Accurate business details, approved copy, brand assets, suitable images, links and any required platform access.

Does it include a domain and hosting?

We can connect them, but registration, hosting plans and third-party charges remain external unless confirmed otherwise in writing.

Can it have more pages or languages?

The free scope is one page in one language. Extra pages and languages can be quoted separately.

Can I remove the Studio 17 credit?

The “Website by Studio 17” credit is part of the free offer unless a different written agreement is made.

Does the cookie setup guarantee compliance?

No. We implement basic controls and approved text; the client remains responsible for appropriate legal advice and accurate information.

What happens after I apply?

We review fit, content readiness, technical needs and capacity. If selected, we confirm responsibilities and external costs before starting.
