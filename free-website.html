Questions before applying.

Know what the offer covers, what it does not cover and what happens after you apply.

Is the website really free?

For selected businesses, Studio 17 waives the design and development fee for the agreed one-page scope. Domain, hosting, licences, paid tools, future updates and work outside that scope are not automatically included.

Who can apply for a free website?

Businesses and credible projects that need a professional first website may apply. We assess whether the need fits a one-page solution, whether the content is ready and whether we have capacity. An application does not guarantee selection.

What do I need to provide?

You provide accurate business details, approved copy, logo and brand assets, suitable images, contact and social links, and any access needed for domain, analytics, Search Console or maps setup.

Does it include a domain and hosting?

The setup can connect your approved domain and hosting, but registration, hosting plans and other third-party charges are external costs unless Studio 17 confirms otherwise in writing.

Can the free website have more pages or languages?

The free scope is one page in one agreed language. Extra pages, translation, localisation and additional-language setup can be quoted separately.

Can I remove the Studio 17 footer credit?

The “Website by Studio 17” credit is part of the free offer and remains visible in the website footer unless a different written agreement is made.

Does the cookie setup guarantee legal compliance?

No. We implement basic controls for the agreed website tools and can publish approved legal text. The client is responsible for obtaining suitable professional advice and providing accurate privacy, cookie and business information.

What happens after I apply?

We review the business, its goal, content readiness, technical needs and current capacity. If the project fits, we confirm the scope, responsibilities, external costs and delivery plan before starting.

Could this be the website your business needs?

Tell us what you do, who the page is for and what a visitor should do next. We will review whether the project fits the free one-page scope.
