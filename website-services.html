Questions businesses ask before choosing a website service

Clear answers to the decisions that usually matter before a project begins

How much does a website cost?

Cost depends on the number of pages, complexity, content, integrations and level of custom work. Our website packages provide clear starting points, and we recommend the smallest useful scope before pricing the work.

Do I need a completely new website?

Not always. If the existing foundation is sound, focused changes to structure, design, content, SEO or performance may solve the problem more efficiently than a rebuild.

Can Studio 17 improve my existing website?

Yes. A website revamp can improve priority pages and customer journeys while preserving the parts that still work.

How long does a website take?

Timing depends on the agreed scope and how quickly content, feedback and access are provided. We confirm a realistic delivery plan before work starts.

Do you provide SEO?

Yes. Every build includes SEO, GEO and technical SEO foundations. Deeper research, content and ongoing optimisation can be added through our SEO services.

Can you create multilingual websites?

Yes. We can plan the content, routing, metadata and layouts required for multilingual and right-to-left experiences.

What happens after launch?

We complete launch checks, connect agreed measurement tools and provide the handover or continuing support defined in the project.

Can you maintain the website?

Yes. Maintenance can cover updates, monitoring, backups, content support, performance and ongoing improvements.

What if I don't know which service I need?

Tell us what is happening. We will review the situation and recommend the smallest useful scope instead of automatically proposing a complete rebuild.
