How much does a website cost?

A simple website at Studio 17 starts at €450. What affects the cost of website development?

Page count, copywriting, language versions, design complexity and integrations such as booking, checkout or CRM connections change development costs. A clear list of needed features lets Studio 17 distinguish a standard package from custom work.

Booking functionality is priced around the reservation system and website scope. Google Ads has two separate costs: the advertising budget paid to Google and the work to set up, manage and measure the campaign.
