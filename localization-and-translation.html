A direct translation can be correct and still feel wrong

Language is only one part of the experience. Search intent, terminology, layout, trust signals and the next action must also make sense in the target market.

The wording sounds translated

Literal phrasing can make an otherwise credible business feel distant or difficult to trust.

The page loses its structure

Longer text, new punctuation and right-to-left languages can break hierarchy, controls and responsive layouts.

The market searches differently

A translated keyword is not automatically the phrase customers actually use when they search and compare.

Updates become inconsistent

Without a clear source, review and publishing workflow, languages quickly drift away from the approved offer.

Translation carries the meaning Localization makes it work

Translation changes the language. Localization adapts the complete experience so it reads naturally, answers the right questions and works technically for the audience using it.

Questions before localizing your website or content

Scope, language expertise and technical requirements are confirmed before work begins.

What is the difference between translation and localization?

Translation changes content from one language to another. Localization also adapts tone, terminology, search intent, formats, calls to action and the digital experience for the target market.

Which languages can Studio 17 support?

Language coverage is confirmed for each project based on the subject, market and required review. We define the appropriate translation and native-review workflow before committing to a scope.

Can you localize an existing website?

Yes. We can review the current structure, prepare the source content, adapt the approved pages and implement the technical language setup without automatically rebuilding the complete website.

Does localization include multilingual SEO?

It can. Multilingual SEO requires market-specific research, localized metadata, suitable landing pages and correct international targeting rather than a translated keyword list.

Can you work with translations we already have?

Yes. We can implement client-supplied translations and separately review consistency, layout, metadata and customer journeys. Language accuracy remains subject to the agreed review scope.

Do you support right-to-left languages?

Yes, where confirmed in the project scope. Right-to-left delivery requires interface mirroring, mixed-language checks, responsive QA and more than changing the text direction.

How long does website localization take?

Timing depends on the number of pages and languages, source readiness, specialist review, platform access and feedback. We confirm milestones after reviewing the actual content and technical setup.

How much do localization and translation services cost?

Pricing depends on content volume, language pair, specialist subject matter, SEO research, technical implementation and review requirements. We recommend the smallest useful scope after reviewing the project.

Ready to make your business feel local in a new market?

Tell us which content, languages and markets are involved. We will help define the right translation, localization and technical scope before recommending the work.
