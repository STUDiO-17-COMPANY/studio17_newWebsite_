A clear process from scope to launch

Good website work depends on decisions being made in the right order.

  1. Discover

    We clarify the audience, offer, goals, content, technical needs and definition of success.

  2. Structure

    We plan the information architecture, page journeys, search intent and conversion actions.

  3. Design & build

    We create the visual system and responsive website, then connect the approved functionality.

  4. Launch & improve

    We complete quality checks, support launch and identify the next evidence-based improvements.

Questions before choosing a package

The package is the starting scope. The proposal confirms exactly what is included for your project.

Are SEO, GEO and technical SEO included in every package?

Yes. Every website build includes the foundations for search visibility, AI-answer understanding and technically clean crawling and indexing. Deeper audits, content strategy or ongoing optimisation can be added where needed.

Which package should I choose?

Choose the package closest to your current page count, audience journeys and functionality. If the fit is unclear, talk to sales and we will recommend the smallest scope that can solve the actual need.

What happens if I need more pages or integrations?

Additional pages, booking, payments, CRM connections, multilingual setup and other add-ons can be scoped separately. You receive that scope before the project begins.

Do the package prices include third-party tools?

No. Licences, domains, hosting plans, payment-provider fees and other paid third-party services are identified separately so ownership and recurring costs stay clear.

How long will the website take?

The timeline depends on scope, content readiness, feedback and integrations. We confirm milestones and responsibilities in the proposal instead of promising one timeline for every project.

Can Studio 17 maintain the website after launch?

Yes. Website Care services are available for technical maintenance, monitoring, content support, performance and ongoing improvements.

Ready to build a website that moves the business forward?

Tell us what the website needs to achieve. We will recommend the right package—or define a custom scope when none of them fits.
