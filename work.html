Questions about our work and client projects

What kind of client work is shown here?

The page brings together selected website, design and marketing work that can be shared publicly. Each link takes you to the most useful available destination: a live website, article, case study or social profile.

Can I see examples for my industry?

Yes. Tell us your industry and the problem you are trying to solve. We can share the closest relevant public examples and explain which parts of the approach would transfer to your situation.

Do all projects have a public case study?

No. Some work is confidential, still in progress or better understood through the live result. We only publish project information that the client has approved for public use.

Can Studio 17 improve work created by another provider?

Yes. We can review an existing website, campaign or customer journey and recommend the smallest useful improvement instead of automatically replacing everything.

Do you work with businesses outside Cyprus and Portugal?

Yes. Studio 17 works across markets and can plan multilingual delivery, remote collaboration and localisation when a project needs to serve more than one country.

Can one project combine several services?

Yes. A project can connect website development, SEO, content, social media, advertising or digital systems when those parts support one business objective.

Can you share confidential work privately?

Where our agreements permit it, we can discuss additional examples during a sales conversation without publishing protected client information on the website.

How do we start a project?

Contact us with the business problem, current situation and desired outcome. We will identify the useful next step, clarify scope and explain what would happen before any work begins.
