What is the difference between short-form and long-form video editing?

Short-form editing usually delivers one focused message quickly, with a strong opening and readable captions. Long-form editing needs sustained structure, pacing and continuity across a longer story. The source footage, graphics and sound requirements determine the work involved in either format.

What graphic design materials does a small business need?

Start with the materials customers actually encounter: a usable logo, clear colours and typography, website assets, social templates and any essential sales documents. Add print or campaign assets when there is a real distribution plan. A smaller consistent set is more useful than a large collection nobody maintains.

Can you redesign existing marketing materials to match brand guidelines?

Yes. Existing assets can be updated to use the correct logo, colours, typefaces and layout rules. Supply editable files and approved guidelines where available. The work may be a straightforward refresh or a fuller redesign if the content and structure also need changing.

What is the difference between digital design and graphic design?

Graphic design covers visual communication across print and digital formats, such as posters, social graphics and brand materials. Digital design can also involve interfaces, interaction and responsive screen layouts. The terms overlap; the important distinction is the asset or experience your project needs.

How does good digital design support conversion?

Good digital design makes the offer, supporting information and next action easy to understand. Readable layouts, visible buttons and usable forms can remove obstacles between interest and enquiry. Measure the customer journey to see whether the changes improve conversions.

What is a hook in short-form video scripting?

A hook is the opening that gives the intended viewer a reason to keep watching. It might show a recognisable problem, demonstrate a result or ask a relevant question. It should match what the video actually delivers, rather than attract attention with a misleading promise.

When should businesses use AI-generated marketing visuals?

AI-generated visuals can suit concepts, stylised campaigns and ideas that would be impractical to photograph. Use real or carefully verified imagery when customers need an accurate view of a product, property or person. Review every asset for brand accuracy, misleading details and appropriate rights before publication.

How can brands maintain consistency with AI-generated content?

Keep an approved visual reference for colours, typography, composition and the appearance of actual products. Review every AI-generated asset against those references and check for invented packaging, distorted details or misleading claims before publishing it.

What are the limitations of AI-generated product images?

AI can invent product features, alter packaging, distort text or show materials inaccurately. These errors can mislead customers even when the image looks convincing. Compare generated images with approved product references and use real photography when accurate representation cannot be assured.
