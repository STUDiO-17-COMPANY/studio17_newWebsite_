44 questions in SEO

What is included in an SEO audit?

An SEO audit examines crawl access, indexing, redirects, page performance, content relevance and internal links. It identifies and prioritises issues; implementing the fixes is a separate stage when not included in the agreed scope.

SEO
How much copy does a service page need?

Use enough copy to explain the service, who it is for, what is included and the questions a customer needs answered before enquiring. Add proof and practical detail where they help the decision. There is no universal word count; remove repetition and expand missing information.

Website Development
What is SEO and why does my business need it?

SEO improves how well a site can be crawled, understood and matched to searches for its services. It involves useful content, technical fixes and relevant local information rather than a guaranteed ranking.

SEO
How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO typically takes sustained work over months because search engines need to crawl changes and competitors keep improving. A technical fix may be measurable sooner than growth in competitive service searches.

SEO
What is included in a technical SEO audit?

A technical SEO audit checks whether search engines can access and index important pages, and investigates redirects, canonical URLs, Core Web Vitals and other implementation issues. The outcome should identify actionable priorities rather than just a score.

SEO
How do I know if Google has indexed my website?

Use Google Search Console's URL Inspection tool to check whether a page is indexed and which canonical Google selected. An XML sitemap helps discovery but does not guarantee indexing.

SEO
Why is my website not appearing in Google?

A website may be missing from Google because its pages are new, blocked, marked noindex, assigned to another canonical URL or not relevant enough for the search being tested. Google Search Console's URL Inspection helps distinguish an indexing issue from a ranking issue. Studio 17 can investigate technical access and content gaps before recommending changes.

Can you fix technical SEO issues on my website?

Yes. Technical fixes can include redirects, sitemap problems, blocked pages, metadata, performance or structured data. The issue list should determine which fixes matter most rather than applying a generic checklist.

How does local SEO help businesses in Cyprus?

Local SEO helps customers in Cyprus find relevant nearby businesses through Google Search and Maps. It connects accurate business details, an eligible Google Business Profile, genuine reviews and useful service and location content. Relevance, distance and prominence influence local results, so a Limassol business cannot guarantee the same visibility across every city.

How does local SEO help businesses in Portugal?

For Portugal, local SEO should reflect Portuguese search wording, the locations actually served and accurate business contact information. Pages for different cities need a real reason to exist.

SEO
What is schema markup?

Schema markup describes content and business entities using machine-readable data. It can clarify page information for search engines but does not guarantee rich results or AI citations.

SEO
How do Core Web Vitals affect website performance?

Core Web Vitals measure loading, responsiveness and visual stability. Improving them makes pages easier to use; a good score alone is not a substitute for relevant content.

SEO
Can a website redesign hurt SEO?

Yes. A redesign can damage SEO if important pages disappear, addresses change without redirects or search metadata is lost. Compare old and new URLs before launch and monitor afterwards.

SEO Website Development
Do I need a blog to improve SEO?

A blog is useful when it answers customer questions your service pages cannot cover fully. Publishing more articles is not automatically better than improving an unclear service page.

SEO
What is the difference between SEO and paid search?

SEO improves visibility in unpaid search results through useful content, technical access and relevant authority. Paid search buys advertising placements and charges against an ad budget. SEO generally develops over time; ads can bring traffic sooner while funded, and both need landing pages that turn relevant visits into useful actions.

SEO
Do I need SEO if my website is already live?

A live website is only the starting point. Check whether important pages are indexed, explain the services clearly and match what prospective customers search for. If those foundations work, ongoing SEO can focus on relevant opportunities rather than changing pages without evidence.

SEO
What factors influence search rankings?

Search rankings depend on how well a page answers the query, the quality and reliability of its information, technical access and the competitive results available. Location and context can also affect what people see. No single score, keyword placement or backlink guarantees a ranking.

SEO
What are crawl errors and how are they fixed?

Crawl errors occur when a search engine cannot retrieve a URL, for example because of a server failure, a broken internal link or an access problem. Check the affected URL, server response and Search Console evidence first. Fix the cause, update links or redirects where appropriate, then monitor recrawling.

SEO
Why do site speed and mobile usability matter for SEO?

A slow or difficult mobile experience can stop visitors reading, enquiring or buying. Core Web Vitals help identify loading, responsiveness and visual-stability problems, but a good score alone cannot secure rankings. Prioritise problems affecting real users alongside useful content and technical access.

SEO
How can my business rank for searches near me?

Complete an eligible Google Business Profile with accurate services, categories, contact details and hours, then support it with useful local website content and genuine reviews. Google considers relevance, distance and prominence. You cannot control a searcher's distance or guarantee a position for every 'near me' search.

SEO
Does my business need location-specific landing pages?

Create location pages when each answers a real local need, such as different service areas, premises or market-specific information. Each page should explain what is available there and provide relevant proof and contact details. Replacing a city name in otherwise identical text gives customers little additional value.

SEO
How do local citations affect visibility?

Local citations are references to your business details on directories and other websites. Accurate, relevant listings can help people find the business and corroborate its information. Prioritise credible sources and correct outdated details rather than buying large quantities of low-quality listings.

SEO
What is an SEO content strategy?

An SEO content strategy maps relevant customer searches to useful service pages, location pages and supporting articles. It identifies what already exists, what needs improvement and where genuinely new content is justified. Priorities should reflect commercial relevance and evidence, with measurement after publication.

SEO
How do you choose keywords for a business website?

Keyword selection starts with what customers actually search for, not a list of the biggest-volume phrases. Studio 17 can combine Search Console queries, customer enquiries and keyword research to identify topics with relevant intent, then map each one to a service page or supporting article. For example, 'SEO services Cyprus' has a different buying intent from 'what is technical SEO'.

SEO
Should I create service pages or blog articles first?

Usually, make the pages explaining what you sell clear and complete first. Articles can then answer deeper questions and link readers to the relevant service. If a useful service page already exists, improve it rather than publishing another article for the same buying intent.

SEO
How do I target customers in multiple countries with SEO?

Research how customers search in each target market, then create suitable language or regional versions of the relevant pages. Use a clear URL structure and appropriate language annotations, with locally useful content and conversion paths. Translation alone does not establish demand or relevance in a new country.

SEO
What are hreflang tags?

Hreflang annotations identify alternate language or regional versions of equivalent pages. They help Google choose a suitable version for a searcher, but they do not guarantee rankings or replace a clear website structure. Each version still needs accessible content and appropriate canonical handling.

SEO
Should international websites use subfolders or separate domains?

Language subfolders can keep publishing and maintenance within one website. Separate country domains can support strongly independent markets but require more administration and ongoing resources. Choose around the business structure, market needs and ability to maintain each version, rather than assuming one setup always ranks better.

SEO
How often should a website be audited for SEO?

Run a focused check after a migration, redesign or major technical change, and investigate unexpected indexing or traffic problems when they appear. Broader audits should follow the site's size, update frequency and business importance. Continuous monitoring can catch problems between full reviews.

SEO
What should I do after an SEO audit?

Turn the findings into a prioritised list with evidence, an owner and a defined fix for each important issue. Address blockers and dependencies first, then test the changes and monitor relevant outcomes. A completed audit is a diagnosis; progress depends on implementing useful recommendations.

SEO
How do I optimise my Google Business Profile?

Use the real business name, appropriate categories, accurate hours, contact details and the correct address or service area. Add useful photos and service information, keep special hours current and respond constructively to reviews. Verify eligibility and ownership; extra keywords in a business name are not a substitute for accurate information.

How can a business improve its Google Maps presence?

Start with an eligible, verified Google Business Profile and complete, accurate business information. Choose relevant categories, keep hours current and make it easy for customers to leave genuine reviews. Maps results also depend on distance and prominence, so optimisation cannot guarantee first place.

Can Google Business Profile updates help local discovery?

Updates can keep offers, news and practical information current for people who view your profile. They should complement accurate business details, appropriate categories, photos and genuine reviews. Posting updates alone does not guarantee a higher position in local results.

SEO
How can I improve my business's online reputation?

Improve reputation by delivering good experiences, collecting genuine feedback and addressing issues transparently.

How should businesses respond to negative reviews?

Respond calmly, acknowledge the issue and move sensitive details into a private support channel. Do not disclose personal information.

How do customer reviews affect trust and local visibility?

Genuine reviews help prospective customers assess service quality and can contribute to local prominence. Make it easy for customers to share honest feedback and respond to concerns constructively. Avoid fabricated reviews or incentives that undermine authenticity and platform compliance.

What is digital PR in SEO?

Digital PR earns relevant coverage and references through useful stories, expertise or research rather than mass-produced link placements.

What makes a backlink valuable?

A valuable backlink comes from a relevant, credible page and is naturally earned or editorially justified.

Can digital PR help improve brand discovery in AI search?

Credible third-party mentions may help systems corroborate brand information. There is no guaranteed link between PR and AI citations.

SEO
What is conversion-focused website copywriting?

Conversion-focused copy helps visitors understand what is offered, why it is relevant and what to do next. It answers practical questions about scope, value and trust using specific information. For a service page, that might mean clear deliverables, useful proof and a relevant enquiry action.

Website Development
What is the difference between SEO copywriting and advertising copywriting?

SEO copywriting connects useful page content with what people are searching for. Advertising copy delivers a focused message within a campaign and placement, often with tighter space and a more immediate action. Both need an accurate offer and wording appropriate to the audience.

What is the difference between translation and localisation?

Translation carries meaning from one language to another. Localisation also adapts terminology, examples, formatting, search language and the customer journey for the intended market. A translated page may still need layout and technical checks before it is ready to use.

Localisation & Translation
Why should marketing content be localised for different markets?

People in different markets may search, compare offers and respond to messages differently. Localisation adapts wording, context, formats and calls to action so the offer feels clear and relevant. Keep the underlying business facts consistent while adapting how they are communicated.

Localisation & Translation
Can you translate website content while preserving SEO?

Yes. Studio 17's localisation service can combine reviewed translations with market-specific search research, metadata and technical language setup. Relevant pages need suitable URLs, internal links and hreflang where appropriate. Preserving meaning does not guarantee unchanged rankings, so performance should be monitored.
