What is the difference between a landing page and a business website?

A landing page is built around one offer and one main action, such as requesting a quote or booking a consultation. A business website usually has separate pages for services, company information and contact details, supporting more than one customer journey. A landing page can also sit within a larger website for a specific campaign.

How many pages does a small business website need?

Start with the information customers need to understand your offer and take action. One focused service may fit on a single page; several services, locations or audiences usually need separate pages. Studio 17's Starter Pack supports up to six pages, but useful content should determine the structure.

Should I choose a custom website or a website builder?

A website builder can suit a straightforward site when its templates, features and editing tools meet your needs. A custom build offers more control over design, customer journeys and integrations, but usually requires more development support. Compare the full cost of setup, subscriptions, maintenance and future changes.

When should I redesign my business website?

Check mobile usability, page speed, content accuracy and the enquiry journey first. An audit may reveal that focused fixes are enough without replacing the whole website.

Can a website revamp improve conversions?

Yes. A revamp can improve the path to enquiry or purchase by clarifying the offer, making useful proof visible and simplifying forms or navigation. Compare conversion rates and lead quality before and after the changes. More polished visuals alone will not solve an unsuitable offer or irrelevant traffic.

How can I redesign my website without losing traffic?

Inventory your existing pages, identify which attract useful traffic and preserve their content and URLs where possible. Map changed addresses to relevant new pages with redirects, retain important metadata and test internal links before launch. Monitor indexing and landing-page performance afterwards; no migration can promise zero fluctuation.

What makes a website design trustworthy?

A trustworthy website makes the business easy to identify and contact, explains its offer clearly and uses credible customer proof. Accurate service information, readable layouts, real imagery and reliable forms matter more than decorative effects. Avoid unsupported claims and keep practical details current.

Website Development
What is mobile-first website design?

Mobile-first design starts with the tasks people need to complete on a phone, then adapts the layout to larger screens. It prioritises readable text, useful navigation, touch-friendly controls and forms that are easy to complete. Testing should cover real journeys, not just whether the page fits the screen.

Website Development
How does website UX affect sales?

UX affects how easily visitors understand the offer, compare options and complete a purchase or enquiry. Confusing navigation, unexpected costs or difficult forms can interrupt that journey. Improve the specific points of friction and measure the effect; good UX supports sales alongside pricing, demand and service quality.
