There is no ideal posting frequency for every business. Start with a schedule your team can maintain, choose formats that suit your audience and measure enquiries alongside reach. A consistent, relevant calendar is more useful than posting daily without a purpose.

Measure social media performance against the goal: audience reach for awareness, relevant engagement for content, and qualified enquiries or sales for lead generation. Website tracking and CRM follow-up help distinguish popular posts from commercial results.
