What does social media management include?

The scope can include strategy, content planning, design, video, copy, approvals, publishing, community support and reporting. We define the smallest useful combination after reviewing the business and its channels.

How much do social media services cost?

Pricing depends on the channels, publishing volume, production needs, community workload, advertising and reporting scope. We review what you actually need before recommending a plan.

Which social media platforms do you manage?

Platform selection depends on where your audience is active and what the business needs to achieve. We can support relevant channels such as Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and other agreed platforms.

Can Studio 17 create photos, videos and designs?

Yes. Content production can include photography, filming, video editing, graphic design, copywriting and approved AI-assisted production where suitable.

How often should my business post?

There is no useful universal number. The right rhythm depends on the platform, content quality, audience, resources and campaign goals. Consistency and relevance matter more than publishing filler.

Are paid social media ads included?

Paid campaigns can be added as a separate agreed scope. Organic management and advertising should share the same audience, offer and creative direction even when they are priced separately.

Can you improve social media we already manage?

Yes. We can audit the existing activity, retain what works and improve the strategy, content system, publishing process or measurement without automatically replacing everything.

How long does social media growth take?

There is no responsible fixed timeline or guaranteed follower number. Progress depends on the starting position, audience, content, offer, distribution and consistency. We focus on useful signals tied to business goals.

What if I do not know which service I need?

Tell us what is happening with your channels. We will review the situation and recommend the smallest useful next step instead of automatically proposing a large retainer.
