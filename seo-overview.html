SEO FAQs

What is SEO, and why is it important for my business?

Search engine optimisation makes your website easier to find through search engines like Google. It helps you attract relevant visitors without paying for every click.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Some improvements may become visible within weeks, but meaningful growth often takes several months. Timing depends on your industry, competition, website condition and strategy.

How much do SEO services cost?

Pricing depends on your goals, website size, competition and the services required. We provide tailored proposals with clear deliverables and transparent pricing.

Can SEO help my business get more customers?

SEO can attract people already looking for what you offer. A well-optimised website can then help turn those visits into enquiries and sales.

What’s the difference between SEO and AI SEO?

Traditional SEO focuses on search engines such as Google and Bing. AI SEO focuses on making your business easier to discover and reference in AI-powered answers. Both share technical, content and authority foundations.

Do I need SEO if I already run Google Ads?

Ads can deliver immediate paid visibility, while SEO builds organic visibility over time. Both can help you reach customers at different stages of their buying journey.

Can you optimise my existing website?

Yes. We can identify technical and content opportunities and implement improvements without necessarily rebuilding the entire website.

Do you provide SEO for businesses in different countries?

Yes. We support international and multilingual SEO strategies across different countries, languages and markets.
