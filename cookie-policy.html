Studio 17 uses a small number of browser-storage technologies. Essential preferences support language and consent choices. Optional Google Analytics technologies remain off unless you choose to allow them.

1. Cookies and local storage

Cookies are small text files placed on a device by a website. Local storage is a browser feature that lets a website keep a preference on that device. Both can remember information between visits, but they are controlled and cleared through your browser.

We classify these technologies as essential or functional when needed to remember a choice, and analytics when used to measure website performance.

3. Optional analytics

If you allow analytics, the site loads Google Analytics 4 using measurement ID G-GVWS39DSNX. Its standard implementation may collect page views, user and session statistics, approximate geolocation, and browser and device information. Google states that IP addresses are used at collection time for location and then discarded before being logged.

Advertising storage, Google signals and advertising-personalisation features are disabled in our implementation. If analytics storage is denied, the Google tag is not loaded by this website and analytics cookies are not created by us.

4. Managing or withdrawing consent

You can accept or refuse analytics when the consent panel appears. Refusing does not prevent you from using the website. You can change your choice at any time using in the footer or on this page.

Withdrawing consent updates the consent state and the site attempts to delete its first-party Google Analytics cookies. You can also delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Blocking essential browser storage may mean your language or consent choice is not remembered.

5. External websites

Links to Google Drive, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, partner sites and other external services take you away from Studio 17. Those services may use their own cookies or tracking technologies under their own policies. They are not controlled by this Cookie Policy.

6. Updates and contact

We may update this policy if the website’s technologies or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the current version. For questions, email contact@studio17.world or read our Privacy Policy.
