The Studio 17 Story.

Built because the traditional agency model felt incomplete.

How one observation became a multidisciplinary studio connecting creativity, technology and business systems.

Read the story
01

Built because the traditional agency model felt incomplete.

Studio 17 started with a simple observation.

Most businesses did not have a lack of suppliers. They had too many of them.

One company built the website. Another managed social media. Someone else ran advertising. A freelancer created the branding. Another provider managed the CRM. Developers handled integrations, while the business owner was left trying to make all of those pieces work together.

Each part could be good individually and still produce a poor overall result.

The problem was rarely just the website, the advertising campaign or the content.

The problem was the connection between them.

That idea became the foundation of Studio 17.

02

From creative work to connected business systems.

Studio 17 grew from the belief that creative, marketing and technology decisions should never exist in isolation.

A website should understand where its traffic comes from.

Advertising should connect to a clear customer journey.

Content should support positioning, sales and discovery.

A CRM should reflect the way the company actually sells.

Automation should remove unnecessary work rather than create more complexity.

And every system should ultimately contribute to a real business objective.

“What are you trying to achieve, and what is stopping you from getting there?”

Sometimes the answer is branding. Sometimes it is a new website. Sometimes it is advertising, content, SEO, a CRM, an automation or an internal tool. And increasingly, it is a combination of several of them.

Studio 17 was built to connect those disciplines.

03

Why “Studio” matters.

We deliberately think of Studio 17 as more than a conventional marketing agency.

A studio creates. It experiments. It brings different disciplines together. And it builds around the project rather than forcing every project into the same predefined package.

That philosophy shaped the company as our capabilities expanded across branding, design, development, marketing, photography, video, advertising, SEO, automation, analytics, CRM systems and artificial intelligence.

The objective was never to offer the longest possible list of services.

It was to develop enough capability to solve the entire problem when the problem crosses multiple disciplines.

04

Portugal and Cyprus.

Studio 17 has roots on opposite sides of Europe.

Portugal shaped much of our early creative and commercial experience, while Cyprus became the base from which Studio 17 developed into an international operation.

Today, our presence across both markets gives us an unusual perspective.

We work with different languages, industries, customer behaviours and levels of digital maturity. A local automotive business in Portugal can face completely different challenges from a hospitality company in Cyprus, a European technology company or an international real-estate project.

The market changes. The principle does not.

Understand the business first. Then build what it actually needs.
05

Technology became part of the creative process.

As Studio 17 evolved, technology became increasingly central to the way we worked.

Websites became platforms. Marketing became measurable infrastructure. Customer journeys became automated. Data began flowing between tools. Manual processes became workflows.

And the distinction between a marketing problem and a technology problem became increasingly difficult—and increasingly unnecessary—to make.

For us, that convergence was natural.

We were not interested in technology for the sake of technology. We were interested in what it allowed a business to do better, faster or at a larger scale.

06

Then came AI.

Artificial intelligence accelerated a direction Studio 17 was already moving towards.

It introduced new possibilities across research, content, customer support, internal operations, automation, sales, reporting, development and decision-making.

But our view of AI is the same as our view of every other technology: it needs a purpose.

Adding AI to a broken process does not automatically make the process better.

So rather than treating AI as a standalone product or trend, we integrate it where it genuinely improves the wider system.

That can mean an AI agent supporting customers, automated reporting infrastructure, intelligent lead qualification, internal knowledge systems, content workflows or tools designed specifically around the way a company operates.

The technology changes quickly. The objective remains constant: make the business work better.
07

What Studio 17 is becoming.

Studio 17 continues to evolve beyond the traditional boundaries between agency, creative studio and technology partner.

We build brands. We create campaigns. We develop digital products. We engineer automations. We connect systems. We produce content. We analyse performance. And we use AI where it creates a meaningful advantage.

But those capabilities are simply tools.

The real product is the system they create together.

Because we believe the next generation of successful businesses will not be built by collecting more disconnected tools, agencies and subscriptions.

They will be built by making everything work together.

That is what Studio 17 was created to do.
